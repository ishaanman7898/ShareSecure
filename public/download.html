ShareSecure on your desktop

Its own app for Windows, Mac and Linux, with the same encryption as the website. It updates itself.

Download

First launch: on Windows, select More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, open the app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Two ways to use it

  1. With a ShareSecure account. Sign in, or create an account right in the app. No email needed. Your links work any time, even with the app closed.
  2. Privately on your computer. Your files and encryption key never leave it, with no upload limit. Links keep working after you close the app, until they expire, while your computer is on.

You choose the first time you open it, and can switch later from the ShareSecure icon in your system tray or menu bar.

Why an app

The website’s pages, including the code that encrypts your files, come built into the app instead of being downloaded each time you open it, so a compromised server can’t change that code. Prefer a server or Docker? Host it yourself.
