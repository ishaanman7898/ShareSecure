ShareSecure for your desktop

The same end-to-end encryption as the website, in its own app for Windows, Mac and Linux. Free, open source, and it updates itself.

Other downloads

First launch on Windows: choose More info → Run anyway. On a Mac: open the app once, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Signed installers are on the way.

Website or app

The desktop app

ShareSecure running privately on your computer. Files and keys never leave it, files up to 100 MB, no daily limit, free. Links work while your computer is on, until they expire.

The website

Your ShareSecure account, in any browser. No email needed. Links work any time, even with your computer off. Files up to 10 MB. Sign in

Why the app

  • Code you can trust. Everything runs from the app’s own copy on your computer, so no server can change it.
  • Hide files from screenshots. On Windows and Mac, the app can keep view-only files out of screenshots and screen recordings.
  • Stays out of the way. Lives in your tray, opens maximized, keeps your sign-in encrypted on this computer, and updates itself.
  • Send to usernames too. Link your ShareSecure account in Settings to send a file to someone’s account.

Prefer a server or Docker? Host it yourself.
