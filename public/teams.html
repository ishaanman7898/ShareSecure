Teams Beta

Work on files together. A team has one shared inbox, request links with your name, colour and logo, and admins who decide who’s in. Everything is encrypted to the team’s key in someone’s browser: every member can open it, ShareSecure can’t.

  1. Make a teamGive it a name and colour. You’re its first admin.
  2. Add peopleAnyone with a ShareSecure account, by username. Their key is checked against the public key log.
  3. Share and collect filesAdd files for the team, or send out request links so clients and colleagues can upload to the team without an account.

New team

A request link lets anyone, with or without an account, upload files straight to the team. They see the team’s name and logo, not who made the link.
