Privacy policy

Last updated September 24, 2026

The short version

  • We only ask for a username and password. No email, no real name.
  • Files, their names and notes are stored encrypted, and erased after they expire.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies. We don't log IP addresses.
  • The companies that host the site can see your IP address, like any website host.

This covers the ShareSecure website at sharesecure-du8.pages.dev. The desktop app and other copies people run themselves work differently; see section 7.

1. What we store

Your account

Your username, a hash of your password (never the password itself), and when you signed up. If you connect an AI assistant, we also keep a hash of its token.

Files you share

The file, its name and its type, all encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Alongside them we keep the file's size, when it was uploaded, when it expires, and how many times it's been opened. Notes and drawings people add to a file are encrypted too. Links reshared from yours point at the same encrypted file rather than making another copy.

Who uploaded what

Each file you upload is tagged with a code derived from your account using a secret key. It isn't your username or account number. Files an AI assistant shares for you are tagged the same way. The one-time upload link an assistant gets is erased once it's used, or after 30 minutes.

Your daily upload count

To enforce 5 uploads a day, we count the files tagged to your account in the last 24 hours. There's no separate record.

Files sent to you

When someone sends you a file, we store it with its encrypted note and whether you've accepted it. To enforce the sending limits, we also keep a code derived from the sender's account using a secret key (not their username or account number): on the copy until you accept it, and in a log of sends that's erased after 24 hours. Until then, the server can link a copy to the account that sent it.

2. What we don't collect

No email address, phone number or real name. No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and no cookies. ShareSecure's own code doesn't record IP addresses.

3. Services that handle your data

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and handles every request. Like any web host it sees your IP address and basic request details, under its privacy policy.
  • Turso stores the database, including the encrypted files.
  • GitHub hosts the source code and desktop app downloads.
  • AI assistants you connect, like Claude or ChatGPT, see the links, file names and share details they handle for you, under their own privacy policies. When you use a ShareSecure GPT, ChatGPT gives us a temporary link to each file you attach, and we download it from OpenAI to share it.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

4. How long we keep it

  • A file's link stops working the moment it expires. The file and everything stored about it are erased the next time cleanup runs, which happens every time anyone uploads.
  • If you delete a file, or decline one sent to you, it's erased straight away.
  • Your account stays until you delete it. Deleting it from the account menu erases it and every file you've shared from it right away.

5. What's stored in your browser

Your sign-in lasts until you close the browser. So you can find your shares again, the browser keeps a list of them, and the keys that let you delete them, for up to 30 days. It also remembers that you accepted the terms and whether notifications are on. All of this stays on your device, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

6. How your files are protected

Each file is encrypted with its own key, and those keys come from a master key kept on the server. That protects files if the database is ever copied, but it isn't end-to-end encryption. Whoever controls the server could decrypt files while they exist. If that matters for what you're sharing, run ShareSecure yourself so the key stays on your own machine.

See the security page for how to report a problem.

7. The desktop app and self-hosted copies

When you run ShareSecure yourself, your account, files and encryption key stay on your own computer, and we never receive any of it unless you link a ShareSecure account (see below). A few things still reach the internet:

  • If you link a ShareSecure account to send files to usernames, the app signs in to that account with your username and password (the password isn't kept, only the sign-in token). Each file you send to a username is uploaded as an encrypted copy to ShareSecure's servers under that account. It expires when your share does, is deleted when you delete the share or unlink the account, and is covered by the rest of this policy.
  • To make share links work for other devices, the app uses a public relay (localtunnel at loca.lt). The relay can see traffic that passes through it. If you have your own domain, you can turn it off with the USE_LOCAL_TUNNEL setting.
  • The app checks GitHub for updates.

The self-hosted version also removes author and editing details from PDF and Word files when you upload them.

8. Your choices

You can delete any file you've shared at any time from Your shares, and delete your whole account from the account menu. You can also turn off AI assistant access there. To ask what we hold about you, email ishaanmanoor1@gmail.com with your username.

9. Children

ShareSecure isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly let them use it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
