Changelog

Every release, every change — documented publicly.

v1.10.0 September 2026 Latest

New

  • Send to usernames everywhere — Type @username in Send to and the button becomes Send. Afterwards you see who got it and who didn’t. Every share can also be sent later: use Send in Your shares, or Send to someone else in the share’s window, next to the link and QR code.
  • Sending from the desktop app — When ShareSecure runs on your computer, link your ShareSecure account (account menu → ShareSecure account) to send to usernames. An encrypted copy goes to ShareSecure’s servers so people can get it while your computer is off.
  • AI assistants do the whole job — Assistants can share text they wrote, files they pass in the call, and files from a link, and deliver them to usernames, without asking you to upload anything.
  • Text files — Share .txt, Markdown and CSV files, as well as PDF, DOCX and images.

Changes

  • Receiving page removed — The desktop app’s Receiving files page, where people typed your username to send you a file, is gone. People send to your username instead. Requests already waiting can still be accepted or declined.
  • No footer — Privacy and Terms are in the account menu, and in the desktop app’s tray menu.

Security

  • Sign-ins expire after 30 days.
  • Passwords are stored salted, and repeated wrong passwords are slowed down.
  • Tighter rules — A stricter content policy for pages, only a file’s owner can turn on annotations, and uploads without an account are closed off.
  • Sending limits — You can only send files you shared, one person can have at most 5 of your files waiting, and each account can send up to 60 a day.
  • Private uploads turned off — The experimental zero-knowledge uploads are off, because their checker could be fooled with a forged proof. Uploads now always use your normal sign-in.
  • Files sent to you stay private — A file someone sends to your username can’t be turned into a public link.
v1.9.2 September 2026

Fixes

  • Bigger files — Uploads over about half a megabyte could fail on the website, and opening a link sometimes said the file couldn’t be displayed until you reloaded. Uploading, opening and resharing now do far less work, and the viewer retries once if something goes wrong.
  • Deleting a reshared link — Deleting a link someone reshared used to be able to delete the original too. Now deleting a link removes it and the links shared onward from it, and nothing else. Deleting the original still removes every link.
  • Downloads — Files set to view-only can no longer be downloaded directly.

New

  • Claude and ChatGPT apps — Add ShareSecure to Claude as a custom connector, or make a ShareSecure GPT in ChatGPT that shares files you attach. Connect an AI assistant in the account menu has the steps. Assistants that can’t reach your files give you a one-time page to pick the file.
  • Desktop sign-up — The desktop app’s welcome screen now has Sign in and Create an account, and it no longer shows links to download or host ShareSecure.
  • Choose who can open a link — Anyone with the link, as before, or only people signed in to ShareSecure. Someone who isn’t signed in is asked to sign in, then taken straight back to the file.
v1.9.1 September 2026

Fixes

  • Mac app “is damaged” — The Mac app is now signed, so it opens instead of being reported as damaged. The first time, open it, then choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Link created — The link gets its own full-width line instead of being squeezed next to the Copy button, with Copy link and Open underneath. The upload form resets for your next file.

Changes

  • Send to several people — Enter usernames separated by commas, or ask your AI assistant to share with a list of people. Each gets their own copy to accept.
  • Tidier desktop app — Receiving files and Updates moved into the account menu.
  • No upload counter — The daily counter next to Your shares is gone. The limit still applies and you’ll be told if you reach it.
  • Faster — Fonts load from ShareSecure itself instead of Google, the browser keeps the site’s code and fonts between visits, the encryption tools load only when you upload, and uploads make fewer database round trips.
v1.9.0 September 2026

AI assistants

  • Share files from Claude Code, Codex and other MCP clients — Choose Connect an AI assistant in the account menu to get a token and the setup command. Then ask your assistant to share a file and it gives you the link.
  • Desktop — The desktop app runs its own MCP server on this computer, so assistants can share files straight from their path.

Accounts

  • Delete your account — From the account menu on the website, on your phone and in the desktop app. It erases the account and every file shared from it right away.
  • Sign in from the desktop app — On first launch, choose to sign in to your ShareSecure account or run ShareSecure privately on your computer. You can switch later from the tray icon.

Fixes

  • Signed out when creating a link — Accounts created recently were signed out the moment they uploaded, because their private sign-in key was never saved. That's fixed, and affected accounts repair themselves the next time you sign in.
  • File types — The website now checks that uploads really are PDF, Word, PNG or JPG files.
v1.8.0 September 2026

Desktop app

  • A real app — Download ShareSecure for Windows, Mac or Linux from the Host it yourself page. It opens in its own window instead of your browser.
  • Runs in the tray — Closing the window keeps it running, so your links keep working. Quit from the tray icon.
  • Updates itself — New versions download in the background and install the next time you quit.
  • New icon — A vault dial with a timer ring, so you can find it in Start and Spotlight.

Look and feel

  • Moving rosette — On the home page, circle your cursor around the rosette and its rings turn with you.
  • Account menu — Your name, files sent to you, notifications and sign out now live in one menu at the top right.
  • Time left at a glance — Each share shows a bar for how much of its life is left, and it turns red near the end.
  • Phones — Bigger tap targets, no zooming when you type, and “Tap to choose a file” on touch screens.

Fixes

  • Upload counter — “Your shares” now counts every upload today. It used to stay at 0.
  • Daily limit — The 5-a-day limit now covers both ways of uploading, so it can’t be doubled.
v1.7.0 September 2026

File requests

  • Accept or decline — Files sent to your username arrive as requests. You see the name, type, size and an optional note, and nothing can be opened until you accept it. Declining erases the file.
  • Notifications — A bell with a count, a toast when a request arrives, the count in the tab title, and optional system notifications that don’t show file names.
  • Send while uploading — The web version’s upload form has an optional “Send to a user” field.
  • Receiving on the desktop version — Turn on “Let people send me files” to share a /send page where people enter your username to send you a file. It’s off until you turn it on, and at most 20 requests can wait at once.
  • Honest file names — A file’s shown name always ends in its real type, so a PDF can’t arrive looking like an .exe.
v1.6.1 September 2026

Fixes

  • In-app updates on Windows — Updates no longer fail when Git for Windows’ tar comes first on the PATH.
v1.6.0 September 2026

Sign in

  • Sign-in page — A proper page for signing in and creating an account replaces the pop-up.
  • Self-hosted owner account — The desktop version asks you to create an owner account on first run. Only the owner can upload and manage files. Passwords use salted scrypt, sessions are signed and expire, and repeated wrong passwords lock sign-in.

Nothing left behind

  • Erased on expiry — Self-hosted files are erased within 30 seconds of expiring, or immediately when someone opens the dead link. The database uses secure_delete, which destroys each file’s own key, and the stored file is overwritten before it’s removed.
  • Expiry fix — Links that expired earlier the same day were sometimes kept until the next day. Fixed on both versions.
  • Browser cleanup — Expired shares disappear from your list and your browser’s storage on their own.
  • Your data folder — Self-hosted data now lives in your user app-data folder, apart from the program and out of OneDrive.

Updates

  • In-app updates — The desktop version shows when a new release is out. Install it with one click, or turn on automatic updates. ShareSecure restarts itself on the new version.

Viewer and security

  • Simpler viewer — Removed rotate, theme, fullscreen, the badge and the footer. Five pen colours instead of ten; sending to a user moved into the share panel.
  • ZK proof checks — The UniGroth verifier now recomputes its checks itself and binds every opened value to its position and the public inputs.
v1.5.0 September 2026

Self-hosting

  • New self-host page — One-command install for macOS, Linux, Windows and Docker, with the right command picked for your system. Linked from the home page.
  • Docker support — Added a Dockerfile and compose file. The image builds straight from GitHub, and the encryption key lives in the data volume so it survives updates.
  • Installer fixes — Re-running the Windows installer without Git no longer deletes your files, errors no longer close the PowerShell window, and the “start now?” prompt works under curl | bash.
  • Correct share links — Links follow your PORT setting instead of always pointing at port 3000.

Design

  • Black and white — The app, docs and viewer are now pure monochrome.
  • One-screen home page — The landing page fits the window without scrolling on desktop and phones.
v1.4.0 September 2026

Redesign

  • New look — Rebuilt interface with a new rosette mark and a single typeface across the app, docs and viewer.
  • Light and dark mode — The app and docs pages follow your system setting.
  • Cleaner upload flow — Simpler form with switches for annotations and downloads, a calmer file list and a redesigned link modal.
  • Accessibility — Keyboard-operable drop zone, Escape closes dialogs, visible focus rings and labelled controls.

Fixes

  • Filename escaping — File and user names are now HTML-escaped in the dashboard and inbox.
  • Custom expiry message — The warning now states the correct 10-day limit.
v1.2.0 May 2026

New Features

  • Image uploads — PNG and JPEG files now accepted alongside PDF and DOCX. Magic byte validation ensures content integrity.
  • Annotation system restored — Per-document drawing toolbar with pen, highlighter, eraser, cursor and 10 colours. Annotations saved server-side, per-link isolated.
  • Annotation keyboard shortcuts — C=cursor, P=pen, H=highlight, E=eraser, Z=undo, X=clear page, 1–0 cycle colours.
  • Two-column dashboard layout — Upload form and file dashboard shown side-by-side after login on desktop.
  • Upload result modal — Success state shown as a popup overlay with link, QR code and actions. Upload form stays accessible behind it.
  • Anonymous file inbox — Send files to registered users without revealing your identity.

Privacy & Security

  • Session-only auth — Auth token moved to sessionStorage. Sessions no longer persist across browser restarts.
  • 10-day expiry cap — Maximum link lifetime reduced to 10 days. Expiry options cleaned up (removed 1 min, 5 min, 14-day, 30-day).
  • No user-file association — Server never stores a user ID next to a file. Dashboard data lives in localStorage only.
  • ZK anonymous uploads — Groth16 zero-knowledge proofs used for rate-limit enforcement without revealing identity.

Viewer Polish

  • Search removed — PDF text search removed to prevent content extraction via search queries.
  • Compact countdown — Timer now shows "4d 2h" / "3h 45m" / "12m 30s" instead of verbose "70h 23m 14s".
  • Fit-to-width icon — Fixed duplicate fullscreen/fit icon. Fit button now uses a distinct expand-diagonal icon.
  • Title truncation — Document title truncated more aggressively to prevent overlap with the countdown timer.
v1.1.0 March 2026

Security & Privacy

  • In-file metadata stripping — Automatic scrubbing of PDF and DOCX metadata (Author, computer, revision info).
  • HMAC integrity hashes — HMAC-SHA256 keyed with a secret salt prevents correlation with public file databases.
  • Security headers — Full suite of Referrer-Policy, nosniff, Frame-Options, and Permissions-Policy.
  • Reduced database footprint — Removed short_id from upload logs to break user→file association.

Bug Fixes

  • Upload quota bypass closed — Quotas now persist even if a file is immediately deleted.
  • DOCX Viewer — Fixed rendering of Word documents using Mammoth.js for client-side HTML conversion.
  • Memory leaks — Resolved various TypeError crashes related to DOM replacement.
v1.0.x March 2026 Patches

Features

  • Self-host Admin Mode — Automatic detection and exposure of admin UI for local deployments.
  • Document Rename — Uploaders can set custom display names for their files.
  • Forward Secrecy — Per-file key wrapping for stored content.
  • Screenshot Protection — Blackout behaviors on tab blur or visibility change.
