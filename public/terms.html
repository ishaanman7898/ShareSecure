1. Who runs ShareSecure

2. Your account

You sign up with a username and password. We don't ask for your email, so we can't reset a forgotten password or contact you: keep your password somewhere safe. You must be at least 13. You're responsible for what happens under your account; don't share it or use someone else's. If you connect an AI assistant, anything it shares or sends with your token counts as shared or sent by you. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

3. Your files

You keep all rights to what you upload. You give us permission only to store it, and to show it to the people who open your link, until the link expires or you delete it.

Anyone with a link can open the file until it expires, unless you limit it to people signed in to ShareSecure, add a passcode, or make it work once. People who open your link can share it onward; deleting your link also deletes the links shared from it. If you make a file request, anyone with that link can send you files, and these terms apply to what they send.

4. What you can't do

Don't use ShareSecure to share or do:

  • anything illegal where you or the person receiving it are

  • malware, or anything designed to harm devices or accounts
  • content that infringes someone's copyright or other rights
  • other people's private information without their permission

Don't try to break into accounts, get around limits or the anonymous-token system, or disrupt the service. Security research is welcome if you follow the security page. Don't use bots or scrapers to collect anything from ShareSecure, and don't use anything shared here to train or improve AI models.

5. Features that involve other people

  • Watermarks and screenshot deterrents make leaks traceable and casual copying harder. They're “screenshot-resistant”, not screenshot-proof: browsers can't fully block screenshots, screen recorders or a phone camera, and we don't promise they'll stop a determined person.
  • Sending to usernames: each person gets their own copy to accept or decline. Don't send people things they haven't asked for in bulk.

6. Limits

  • Files can be documents (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument), images, video, audio, ZIP and other archives, or text, up to 10 MB each on the website. Programs and scripts can't be shared on their own.
  • Free accounts can share 5 files and send 60 a day, and keep 10 file requests open. Pro raises these (shown in Settings → Plan).
  • Links expire after 1 hour to 10 days, as you choose. Then the file is erased.

We may change these limits; the current ones are always shown in the app.

A team needs at least one admin on Pro to keep taking files.
